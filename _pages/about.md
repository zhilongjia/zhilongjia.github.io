I am an Associate Professor at Chinese PLA General Hospital after obtaining a doctor of philosophy degree in Biomedical Engineering. My research interests are Omics-based drug repositioning. I developed drug repositioning methods, such as cogena, and applied them into various diseases, such as cardiovascular disease, periodontal disease, mountain sickness and COVID-19.
